## THE BIGKAS PROGRESSIVE ACTIVITIES (B-01 GUIDED FORMAT)

**START OF JOURNEY 1**
**Journey 1: FOUNDATION (30 Stages)**
---

**Stage 01:** "Introduce yourself to the camera. State your full name, your age, and your course."
**B-01’s Purpose:** I ask for your personal details, so you don't overthink. This lets you focus purely on
locking eye contact with the camera while I establish your natural vocal baseline.

**Stage 02:** "Describe the color and design of the shirt you are wearing right now."
**B-01’s Purpose:** Describing your shirt keeps your cognitive load low, preventing early anxiety while I track
your gaze.

**Stage 03:** "Talk about the last meal or snack you ate today. Describe the food."
**B-01’s Purpose:** Talking about food naturally relaxes you, giving me a calm and unforced baseline for your
voice.

**Stage 04:** "Look at the camera and name three items you can see in your room right now. Describe
where they are."
**B-01’s Purpose:** Mentally mapping your room adds minor multitasking, training you to maintain eye
contact even when thinking.

**Stage 05:** "What is your favorite color? Explain briefly why you like it."
**B-01’s Purpose:** Asking "why" encourages you to build simple, logical sentences without the pressure of
right or wrong answers.

**Stage 06:** "Sit up straight and describe the weather outside today. Is it hot, raining, or cloudy?"
**B-01’s Purpose:** Instructing you to adjust your posture gives me a visual baseline for your confident,
upright stance.

**Stage 07:** "Talk about your favorite video game or mobile app. How does it work?"
**B-01’s Purpose:** Explaining game mechanics trains you to maintain physical composure while giving
instructions.
**Stage 08:** "Describe your dream vacation destination. What do you want to see there?"
**B-01’s Purpose:** Imagining a dream vacation naturally opens up your body language, giving me data on
relaxed shoulders.


**Stage 09:** "What is your favorite drink (coffee, milk tea, juice)? Describe how it tastes."
**B-01’s Purpose:** Describing taste requires specific words, letting me track vocabulary while your body
stays relaxed.

**Stage 10:** "Talk about your go-to comfort food when you are stressed."
**B-01’s Purpose:** Discussing stress relief naturally lowers vocal tension, giving me a clean sample of your
most relaxed voice.

**Stage 11:** "Describe your favorite hobby. What do you usually do when you have free time?"
**B-01’s Purpose:** Talking about hobbies provides a steady flow of familiar words, letting me track your
volume consistency safely.

**Stage 12:** "Talk about your usual commute going to the campus. What ride do you take?"
**B-01’s Purpose:** Explaining your commute trains you to sequence events naturally and maintain a steady
speaking pace.

**Stage 13:** "Who is your favorite music artist or band? Explain why you listen to them."
**B-01’s Purpose:** Discussing favorite artists brings out positive emotions, allowing me to measure your
natural pitch variations.

**Stage 14:** "Talk about your favorite subject in school. What makes it interesting for you?"
**B-01’s Purpose:** Shifting to positive school topics establishes how you sound when discussing academics
confidently.

**Stage 15:** "Describe your favorite movie or series in three simple sentences."
**B-01’s Purpose:** The "three simple sentences" rule tests your ability to control and structure your
thoughts concisely.

**Stage 16:** "Talk about a pet you have, or a pet you wish you had. Describe them."
**B-01’s Purpose:** Describing a pet keeps you relaxed, ensuring your voice stays stable and doesn't shake
from nervousness.

**Stage 17:** "Explain the step-by-step process of how you prepare yourself in the morning."
**B-01’s Purpose:** Explaining a step-by-step routine trains you to speak continuously and avoid "dead air."

**Stage 18:** "Talk about a specific place or hangout spot you like visiting with your friends."
**B-01’s Purpose:** Describing a specific location forces you to build sentences continuously without
overthinking.

**Stage 19:** "What is the most useful app on your smartphone right now? Explain how you use it."
**B-01’s Purpose:** Explaining how an app works trains you to communicate technical utility clearly and
casually.

**Stage 20:** "Talk about a physical activity or sport you enjoy watching or playing."
**B-01’s Purpose:** Discussing sports naturally increases your speaking pace, letting me monitor your speed
control.

**Stage 21:** "Describe the best gift you have ever received. Who gave it to you?"
**B-01’s Purpose:** Recalling an event and a person helps you practice fluid storytelling while keeping your
speech continuous.

**Stage 22:** "Share a simple, happy memory from your childhood."
**B-01’s Purpose:** Sharing a happy memory naturally triggers smiles, giving me a benchmark for your
expressive delivery.

**Stage 23:** "Talk about a friend or classmate you admire. What good traits do they have?"
**B-01’s Purpose:** Praising someone raises your vocal pitch and energy, helping me map your dynamic
vocal range.

**Stage 24:** "What is your favorite time of the day (morning, afternoon, or night)? Why?"
**B-01’s Purpose:** Explaining a preference trains you to quickly blend logical structure with personal
opinion.


**Stage 25:** "Describe a simple talent or skill you have that you are proud of."
**B-01’s Purpose:** Speaking about your talents tests your ability to project visual and vocal confidence
without feeling boastful.

**Stage 26:** "What is your favorite animal? Explain what you find fascinating about it."
**B-01’s Purpose:** Explaining a fascination naturally engages your facial expressions, providing great visual
tracking data.

**Stage 27:** "Share one simple goal or task you want to finish by the end of this day."
**B-01’s Purpose:** Stating a daily goal requires a firm tone, preparing your voice for serious projections.

**Stage 28:** "Describe what your ideal weekend rest day looks like."
**B-01’s Purpose:** Describing an ideal weekend lets you speak smoothly from imagination, capturing your
stable posture.

**Stage 29:** "Describe your favorite spot to relax or study on campus."
**B-01’s Purpose:** Keeping the topic on familiar school settings ensures your flow isn't broken by unfamiliar
concepts.

**Stage 30 (Boss Stage):** "You have 60 seconds. Deliver a brief self-introduction mentioning your name and
your course. Explain why good communication skills are important for your future career. Maintain
steady eye contact, good posture, and a clear voice."
**B-01’s Purpose:** This is your Boss Stage. It combines all constraints (eye contact, posture, steady voice) to
prove you can handle a continuous 60-second speaking load before advancing.

**B-01’s Purpose:** Summarizing your week forces you to pace yourself so you don't rush the ending.

**Stage 17:** "Explain how you usually prepare for a major exam. Speak slowly and clearly."

**Stage 18:** "Describe a hobby of yours that requires patience. Let your speaking pace match that feeling of
patience."
**B-01’s Purpose:** Discussing a patient hobby naturally translates to a slower, more deliberate speaking
rhythm.

**Stage 19:** "Talk about a funny moment with your friends. Pause naturally and do not rush the delivery."
**B-01’s Purpose:** Telling a funny story trains you to pause naturally and not rush the delivery.

**Stage 20:** "Describe your favorite quiet place to study. Use a steady pace and do not speed up on familiar
details."
**B-01’s Purpose:** Describing a quiet place helps you maintain a steady pace without speeding up on
familiar details.

**Stage 21:** "Talk about a time you felt extremely exhausted from school. Let your tone reflect being tired."
**B-01’s Purpose:** Recalling exhaustion trains your vocal cords to match a tired emotional context.

**Stage 22:** "Explain a strict rule your parents or school has. Speak with a serious, calm, and firm tone."
**B-01’s Purpose:** Explaining a strict rule forces your tone to become serious, calm, and firm.

**Stage 23:** "Talk about a happy memory with a childhood friend. Use a joyful voice and smile naturally
while speaking."
**B-01’s Purpose:** Recalling a happy memory naturally induces a smile, preventing a robotic tone.

**Stage 24:** "Talk about a pet peeve or something that mildly annoys you. Show controlled frustration in
your voice."
**B-01’s Purpose:** Discussing a pet peeve lets me track controlled frustration in your voice.

**Stage 25:** "Talk about someone who helped you recently. Use a warm, sincere, and grateful tone."
**B-01’s Purpose:** Talking about someone helpful trains your voice to sound warm, sincere, and grateful.

**Stage 26:** "Re-introduce yourself formally to the camera. Keep a steady voice, look at the lens, and do
not let your voice tremble."
**B-01’s Purpose:** Re-introducing yourself tests if you can keep your voice steady while making strong eye
contact.

**Stage 27:** "Describe a small challenge you faced today. Project confidence and keep your voice from
shaking."
**B-01’s Purpose:** Describing a challenge test your ability to project confidence visually while keeping your
voice from shaking.

**Stage 28:** "Express gratitude to a family member. Use a stable voice, natural hand gestures, and maintain
eye contact."
**B-01’s Purpose:** Expressing gratitude trains you to combine a stable voice with natural hand gestures.

**Stage 29:** "Do you think students should have part-time jobs? Speak at a steady pace with absolute
conviction."
**B-01’s Purpose:** Defending a stance on part-time jobs trains you to speak at a steady pace with absolute
conviction.

**Stage 30:** (Boss Stage): "You have 90 seconds. Explain how you have adjusted to your current grade level
or year compared to last year. Demonstrate strong voice projection, pace control, and emotional tone
variation while maintaining steady eye contact. Speak clearly and do not let your volume drop at the end
of your sentences."
**B-01’s Purpose:** This Boss Stage tests your ability to combine strong projection, pace control, and
emotional tone variation simultaneously under pressure.

**Stage 05:** "Talk about a new skill you want to learn. If you accidentally use a filler word, correct yourself immediately and confidently."
**B-01’s Purpose:** Self-correction builds your confidence to recover smoothly when you accidentally slip up.

**Stage 06:** "Describe your favorite fruit and why you like it without using the word 'like' as a filler."

**Stage 07:** "Explain the first thing you do when your morning alarm rings. Use complete silence whenever you need to think."
**B-01’s Purpose:** Using silence instead of fillers helps you gather your thoughts professionally.

**Stage 08:** "Talk about your favorite day of the week and why. Speak with zero hesitation sounds."
**B-01’s Purpose:** Discussing a favorite day tests your fluency without relying on hesitation sounds.

**Stage 09:** "Explain why drinking water is important. Speak carefully and eliminate all 'you knows'."
**B-01’s Purpose:** Banning 'you know' forces you to take responsibility for explaining concepts clearly.

**Stage 10:** "Describe the bag you use for school. Speak deliberately and cleanly."
**B-01’s Purpose:** Describing a school bag deliberately locks in your habit of clean speaking.

**Stage 11:** "Explain how the 'internet' works to a 5-year-old child using simple words."
**B-01’s Purpose:** Explaining to a child forces you to break down technical jargon into simple words.

**Stage 12:** "Describe a complex topic from your specific track, strand, or course using everyday language. Do not use jargon."
**B-01’s Purpose:** Simplifying course concepts tests your ability to adjust your vocabulary for beginners.

**Stage 14:** "Explain your dream career to your grandmother who has never heard of it."
**B-01’s Purpose:** Explaining careers to an elder trains your respectful, accessible vocabulary.

**Stage 15:** "Explain what 'Wi-Fi' is to a child using a very simple, easy-to-understand analogy."

**Stage 16:** "Describe what a smartphone 'app' is to someone from the 1950s who has never seen modern technology."

**Stage 17:** "Explain a common slang word you and your friends use to an elderly person. Be respectful and clear."

**Stage 18:** "Describe your hardest subject in school without using any technical words from that subject."
**B-01’s Purpose:** Removing technical terms from a hard subject proves your true understanding of it.

**Stage 19:** "Describe 'online shopping' using a physical mall or store analogy."
**B-01’s Purpose:** Using a mall analogy for online shopping strengthens your comparative vocabulary.

**Stage 20:** "What does 'Artificial Intelligence' mean? Explain it clearly and kindly to a grandparent."
**B-01’s Purpose:** Explaining AI kindly builds empathy into your communication style.

**Stage 21:** "Why did you choose your current school or track? Structure your answer using: 'First... Second... Finally...'"

**Stage 29:** "What is the single best study habit? Defend your choice strongly. Do not say 'it depends.' Take a stand."

**B-01’s Purpose:** This Boss Stage tests your ultimate ability to structure a past/present/future narrative with zero fillers and perfect eye contact.
